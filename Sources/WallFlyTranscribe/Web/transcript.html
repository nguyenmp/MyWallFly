      How to use this

      • Use the pencil on a line to fix its words.
      • Highlight words, then pick a name to hand them to someone else.
      • Click a speaker on a line to reassign the whole turn.
      • Rename or merge in the bar above.

      Only settled lines take edits. The live line at the bottom stays read-only, because its speaker can still change.
